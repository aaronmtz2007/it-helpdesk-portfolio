# SOP 01: Daily invoice entry (before 10:00 AM ET)

🇪🇸 [Versión en español](01-daily-invoice-entry.es.md)

**Purpose:** get every invoice into the factoring and banking portals before the 10:00 AM ET cutoff, so the payment arrives the same day.
**Owner:** operations coordinator · **Frequency:** every weekday

> Time zone: 10:00 AM ET is 9:00 AM in Campeche in winter and 8:00 AM in summer. The reminder arrives at 9:30 AM ET.

## Steps
1. At 9:30 AM ET the email "N invoice(s) to enter before 10:00 AM ET" arrives (workflow 02). It lists each pending invoice (number, customer, purchase order, amount) and the total.
2. Open the factoring portal and enter each invoice from the list.
3. Open the banking portal and enter the same invoices.
4. Compare the total in each portal with the total in the email. If they don't match, stop and find the missing invoice before the cutoff.
5. Mark each invoice as entered with the form **Invoice entered in the portal** (workflow 06).
6. Check the dashboard: the **Pending invoices** table should be empty.

## If something goes wrong
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| An invoice is missing from the email | Its loads were not billed yet. Follow [SOP 03](03-invoicing-against-a-purchase-order.md) |
| A portal is down | Email the founder before 10:00 AM ET with the invoice list |
| The cutoff was missed | The payment arrives the next business day. Write it down and mention it in the daily summary |
